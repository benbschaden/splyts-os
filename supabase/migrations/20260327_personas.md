# Migration: personas

## Summary
Adds the `personas` table — multiple target audience personas per org, with rich structured fields for demographics, goals, frustrations, behaviours, and AI visibility control.

## Gherkin specs
- `docs/features/personas.md`

## ADRs
- Separate columns (not JSONB) for each field: enables filtering, search, and selective AI inclusion by field later.
- `include_in_ai` boolean per persona: admins can exclude a persona from AI context without deleting it.
- Soft delete with `deleted_at`: consistent with all other content tables.

## Design notes
- No UNIQUE constraint on name — orgs may legitimately have multiple personas with similar names.
- `quote` is a free-text representative quote in the persona's voice, useful for AI calibration.
- All fields except `name` and `created_by` are nullable — admins fill in as much or as little as needed.
